Personal Manifesto
Tidy yourself up. Whatever came today — tomorrow gets another try.

I'm Jiarui (Sunny) Yao — an Interaction Designer in my second year at Sheridan College, Oakville. I design things that make people feel something: a voice-driven ice cream that melts when you shout at it, a city map you can hold in your hands, a landing page that changes your mind before you realise it happened.

I believe the best design is quiet until it isn't. And I believe in showing up the next day.
